# Player photo credits

All player photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licenses. Each file in this folder is a derivative of the original listed below.

**Modified: background removed and cropped by the project author.** The paper border, shadow, rotation and caption are drawn by the app at render time, not baked into the files. `preview.jpg` is a composite of these derivatives.

Files derived from CC BY-SA originals are shared under the same license (CC BY-SA 2.0, or CC BY-SA 4.0 for the Draymond Green file). Files derived from CC BY originals are shared under CC BY 2.0.

No NBA, Getty, AP, or news-agency images are used.
